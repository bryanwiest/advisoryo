How it works

  1. Write

    Publishers create the advisory in an editor such as Secvisogram and export it as CSAF 2.0 JSON.

  2. Upload and validate

    advisoryo checks the document against the official CSAF schema and lists every error in plain language.

  3. Publish and find

    Valid advisories are public right away and searchable by CVE, product, manufacturer, severity and status.

Who it is for

Manufacturers

Meet your disclosure duties under the EU Cyber Resilience Act without running your own CSAF portal. Organisation managers invite publishers and keep the company profile up to date.

Sign in to publish

Operators and security teams

Search all advisories without an account, check which product versions are affected and download the original CSAF document for your own tooling.
